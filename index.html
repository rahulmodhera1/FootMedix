Comprehensive Foot Care Services

From routine maintenance to specialized treatment, we care for every part of your foot health under one roof.

General Foot Health

Routine assessments and maintenance to keep your feet comfortable, healthy and pain-free.

Nail Care

Professional trimming and management of thickened, discoloured or problematic toenails.

Callus & Corn Removal

Gentle removal of thick, hardened skin caused by friction and pressure, restoring comfort.

Custom Orthotics

Custom-made devices that discreetly support, align and accommodate your feet to improve function.

About FootMedix

Expert, compassionate care for your feet

FootMedix is a dedicated chiropody clinic in Scarborough, Toronto, led by Emily, a chiropodist registered with the College of Chiropodists of Ontario. A chiropodist is a primary healthcare professional who specializes in the assessment, management and prevention of diseases and disorders of the foot — through therapeutic, surgical, orthotic and palliative care.

Emily has extensive experience treating podiatric nail and skin disorders, conducting biomechanical and gait analyses, and providing diabetic foot care and education. Every patient receives a thorough, personalized assessment and a treatment plan built around their needs and lifestyle.

  • Registered with the College of Chiropodists of Ontario
  • Personalized, patient-first treatment plans
  • Conservative and surgical treatment options
  • Currently accepting new patients
